# MAT508 Time Series Pack Map

## Pack A: L1–L2

Intro, smoothing, decomposition, SMA, exponential smoothing, Holt-Winters.

## Pack B: L3–L4

Stochastic processes, strict/WSS stationarity, covariance, named processes, short/long memory.

## Pack C: L5–L7

Spectral methods, filtering, periodogram, BLUP, estimation, sample covariance/correlation.

## Pack D: L8–L10

ARMA definitions, backshift notation, causality, invertibility, roots, ARMA properties, covariance/ACF/PACF.

## Pack E: L11–L13

ARMA fitting, Yule-Walker, forecasting, ARIMA, SARIMA.

## Pack F: L14–L15

Real-data workflow, transformations, AIC, forecasting workflow, SSA/Rssa.

## Two-pass strategy

Pass 1:

- understand definitions, formulas, worked examples.

Pass 2:

- exam behaviour: formula recall, standard question templates, timed work, proof skeletons, R-code skeletons.
