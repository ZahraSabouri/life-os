How to use Everyday

Updated 25 September 2026. Open the app at http://127.0.0.1:47841/ using your usual Budget Management launcher.

The three things to keep separate

What you want to recordWhere to goWhat changes
Money you actually paidShopping → Receipts → Add receipt, or Money → Add payment inside Bills & receiptsYour spending, receipt and any stock you choose to track
What you physically haveFood & supplies → What’s leftYour remaining food and supplies
What you want to use in future monthsFood & supplies → Monthly planMonth assignments for stock you already own
A product you might buy againShopping → CatalogueA reusable product entry; adding a catalogue product alone creates no spending or stock

The date selector at the top changes the reporting/planning month. It does not hide food that is still physically at home. “What’s left” includes stock set aside for later months.

1. Your everyday routine

  1. Before shopping, open Food & supplies → What’s left. Check Fridge, Freezer and Cupboard / pantry.
  2. After shopping, record the receipt once. Choose the products, paid amounts and stock quantities for this particular purchase.
  3. After eating or using something, find it in What’s left and press Use some. For a bottle or other long-lasting product, press Update.
  4. When you want a monthly plan, assign only the stock you want to spread across months.
  5. Check Money & saving → My month for your allowance and receipts.

2. Find what is in the fridge or freezer

Open Food & supplies. The first tab is What’s left.

  • Choose Fridge, Freezer, or another location.
  • Use Find something at home to search by product name.
  • Each card shows the recorded remaining quantity, location and purchase date when known.
  • Finished items are hidden here. Their receipts and usage history remain saved.
  • Different purchases and different units have separate cards. One pack and three portions are not added into a misleading “four”.
  • Products such as a wok or storage box appear under Things you keep.
  • Bottles, jars and similar items can show “Unopened”, “In use”, “About half” or “Running low”. These are approximate states, not weighed amounts.

Browse by category shows the full category tree, including empty categories labelled 0 products. Open an empty category and use Add stock if you already own something, or add a catalogue product from Shopping if it is only a future option.

The app knows what has been entered. If you ate something without recording it, the remaining quantity will be too high. Correct the saved amount or record its use; do not enter another purchase to fix stock.

3. Record food or supplies you used

  1. Find the relevant product and purchase in What’s left.
  2. Press Use some.
  3. Enter the quantity in the unit shown, and the date it was used.
  4. Choose I used it or It went off.
  5. Save. The remaining stock decreases, while the paid receipt stays unchanged.

Example: a card says 4 portions left. You eat one portion. Enter 1 portion, not the original pack weight.

If you use something reserved for a later month, the app can take it from that month and shows where it came from. Stock reserved for a planned meal must be released by changing that meal first.

For oil, shampoo and other products tracked approximately, press Update, then record the relevant opened/half/low/finished state. A product is not automatically finished because time has passed.

To correct a mistake immediately, press Undo in the saved notification. For older consumption entries, open the product’s Details → History → Edit, or find the record in Weekly history. Choose the actual date rather than inventing a precise date you cannot remember.

4. Correct an amount or move something to the fridge

Use Correct amount / location on the product card, choose its pack or share, then edit the saved amount or location and save.

For a purchased pack, Full amount is the starting quantity and Left now is the remaining quantity. For an original monthly share, Amount is the size of that remaining share. Use Use some when you want consumption recorded in history.

Example: you move a product from the cupboard into the fridge. Change its location to Fridge. You are moving existing stock, so its receipt must not be entered again.

If an opened product has not been measured, do not assume the original pack weight is still left. The opened peaches are explicitly labelled amount not measured until their amount is established.

5. Change how this purchase is portioned

Use What’s left → Change portions on the purchase you want to reorganise.

Example: you have 500 g mince and divide it into 5 portions:

  1. Set Total remaining in the new unit to 5.
  2. Set Unit to portion.
  3. Choose Whole pieces only.
  4. Set Number of portions or containers to 5.
  5. Choose the location, add an optional note such as “100 g each”, and save.

Alternatively, keep the stock measured in grams: total 500, unit g, tracking Measured amount or fractions, and 5 containers. Each new container then holds 100 g.

This changes only the selected remaining stock. Previously used amounts keep their old units and history. Other purchases keep their own units. The newly arranged stock is available Any time; assign it to months again if you want to.

Changing portions is different from correcting a counting error. When you keep the same unit, the total must remain unchanged. Use Correct amount / location to fix an incorrect amount.

You do not have to keep using the old half-breast or diced-beef portion definitions. A future chicken purchase can stay as a whole pack, be weighed, or be divided however you choose.

6. Keep a constant amount for selected months

Open Food & supplies → Monthly plan → In my monthly plan.

Your existing Morrisons assignments are kept. Ordinary unassigned Lidl purchases do not appear in this plan. They remain in What’s left until you record their use.

To change an existing product:

  1. Press Edit months.
  2. Enter individual monthly quantities, or expand Use the same amount each month.
  3. For a repeating amount, enter the amount, Repeat from, and Repeat through.
  4. Press Fill these months. This fills the form; it does not save yet.
  5. Review the quantities and press Save allocations.

Example: you own 36 tablets and choose 3 per month from October to June. Nine months use 27 tablets, leaving 9 available Any time. The app will reject a plan that allocates more than you own.

The repeated quantity is your choice for this stock and date range. It does not automatically buy more tablets, record them as used, or force the same rule onto future purchases.

To add another product deliberately, open Add stock to my plan, find it, and press Edit months. To remove a product’s plan, choose Clear this product’s month assignments, then save. Clear all month assignments releases the whole plan while keeping stock, receipts and previous usage.

7. Enter a new shopping receipt

Open Shopping → Receipts → Add receipt. You can also use Add payment inside Money’s Bills & receipts section.

  1. Enter the shop, purchase date and amount paid. This active record starts on 8 September 2026.
  2. Add each bought item. Existing catalogue products can be selected by name, including older reference products.
  3. Enter the actual line total, not the unit price when several units were bought. For three items costing 79p each, quantity is 3 and line total is £2.37.
  4. Choose the category. Expand the line’s At home as… section to review its stock settings.
  5. Under Stock for this purchase, choose Keep as bought or Choose amount or portions.
  6. Enter discounts and any charges once. If line totals already include a discount, do not also enter that same discount in Receipt discount.
  7. Check the message The receipt balances, then press Save paid receipt.

Keep as bought uses this line’s quantity and unit. A new chicken purchase starts as packs rather than silently becoming the previous purchase’s half-breast portions.

Choose amount or portions lets you enter, for example, 6 eggs from one pack, 500 g, or 5 portions. This is a choice for this receipt. Use measured tracking when the amount can be fractional.

Use Just the spending for something you do not want to track at home. Add stock is for stock already owned without recording another payment. A receipt photo is optional; attaching it does not automatically read or enter its contents.

Do not enter a full-shop payment and then add its itemised receipt as another payment. The receipt already includes the full payment. Existing September receipts have been reconciled rather than added on top of their old entries.

8. Read Money & saving correctly

My month has two different measures:

  • Left in this month’s budget: the amount left under your chosen monthly cost allocation.
  • What actually happened → Net cash paid: payments that actually left minus money returned.

The saved setup uses a £250 monthly allowance, with the £155.95 Morrisons receipt allocated over six months. It does not also deduct a separate £25 monthly reserve. Adding both would reserve for the same supplies twice.

At this review, September has £330.29 payments, £8.00 returned, and £322.29 net cash paid. Under the saved allocation, £192.58 counts in September, leaving £57.42 of its £250 allowance. That is a planning balance, not a bank-account balance. Net cash paid is £72.29 above £250.

Open a receipt row to see its items. Count this shop over changes how its cost is allocated across months. It does not change the original amount paid. Choosing Just its own month counts the whole shop in the purchase month. Small penny differences between monthly shares come from allocating receipt lines without losing pennies.

These money allocations are independent of the stock quantities in Monthly plan. Eating something does not charge you again.

9. Catalogue, empty categories and pictures

Shopping → Catalogue holds product names, pictures, categories and price history. It does not mean you own everything pictured there.

  • Current plan: products associated with the current setup and recent purchases.
  • Earlier references: earlier product/price references, kept for future selection.
  • Everything: both together.

Empty category families are visible by default. Use Add product, Add a family, or Add a type here to build the catalogue yourself.

An own-brand Morrisons product can be linked to Morrisons. A product sold in different shops can remain available anywhere; each receipt records the shop used on that occasion.

To change a product picture, open Details, then its picture or Edit details. The approved images have been assigned to their matching products and page categories. The companion Pictures to provide.md lists only remaining gaps or optional replacements.

10. Meals, history and backups

Use Meals & recipes → This week to plan a meal or record one you ate. Planned ingredients reserve existing stock. Marking a meal eaten uses its ingredients; do not also record the same ingredients a second time through Use some.

Weekly history shows recorded consumption by week. Food & supplies → Monthly history shows the month calendar. A receipt stays in Shopping/Money even when all its food is finished.

Changes save locally. Settings & backups → Export everything downloads a complete backup. Restore a backup previews a saved copy before replacing the current one. If another tab saved a newer copy, choose Reload saved copy before editing further.

The update also creates a full backup of the app and data in the BudgetManagement backups folder. Do not use “Bring in old spending” merely to make historical catalogue products available; the catalogue already supports those references.

Text for a one-page infographic

Title: Everyday — buy once, track what is left, plan only what you choose

Six panels, in this order:

  1. CHECK — Food & supplies → What’s left → Fridge / Freezer. See recorded remaining amounts.
  2. BUY — Shopping → Receipts → Add receipt. Enter one balanced receipt, once.
  3. PORTION — What’s left → Change portions. Choose units for this purchase only.
  4. USE — Use some / Update. Record the amount and date; your receipt stays unchanged.
  5. PLAN — Monthly plan → Edit months. Repeat an amount if wanted. Lidl stays unplanned until chosen.
  6. REVIEW — Money → My month. Compare monthly allocation with net cash paid. Export a backup.

Side note: Catalogue = products you can choose. Stock = what you have recorded at home. Monthly plan = how you set that stock aside.

Footer: Old references do not create expenses. Empty categories remain visible. Use Undo if you make a mistake.

Design direction: warm off-white background, forest-green headings, readable sans-serif text and the approved domestic photographs. Keep each panel to one action and one short sentence. Use separate labels for pounds, packs, grams and portions; do not depict them as interchangeable.

Your original portions and early use

Open Food & supplies → Monthly plan → Original plan & usage. Search for a product to compare your starting portions with what remains. The original schedule is a reference; change active assignments in In my monthly plan. Finished food stays finished. New purchases keep their own portions.

Remaining-stock cards show Saved shares, for example 4 × 125 g for linguine. Use some deducts the amount you ate; Change portions reorganises the stock you have left.
