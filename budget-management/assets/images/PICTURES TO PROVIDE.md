# Pictures the app still needs

Written 23 September 2026. The point of this list is that you do **not** need 121 product photos.
The app falls back from a product, to the kind it belongs to, to its family. Fill the families
and the shops and the whole app looks finished; after that, product photos are only worth it for
things you want to recognise at a glance.

Everything goes in `assets/images/approved/…` and the app can now read that folder directly
(stop and start the app once to pick that up). Filenames become the label, so name them plainly.

## The house style, so everything matches

The app is warm cream and sage green with a serif for headings. Whether you photograph these or
generate them, keep to one recipe:

- **Background** plain warm cream, about `#F5F1E6`. No worktops, no patterned cloth, no props.
- **Light** soft daylight from the upper left, gentle shadow to the lower right, no hard flash.
- **Framing** square, subject centred, about 15% empty margin all round.
- **Size** 1200 × 1200, JPEG or WebP, under 400 KB.
- **Colour** slightly warm, slightly muted. Nothing neon.
- **Never** text overlays, logos, watermarks, hands, faces, or a busy scene.

One sentence you can reuse for a generator: *"a single [subject] on a plain warm cream background,
soft natural daylight from the upper left, soft shadow, square composition, centred, generous
margin, muted warm colour, no text, no hands, editorial food photography."*

- The Food & supplies shelf: Fresh food, Pantry & freezer, Snacks & drinks, Household,
