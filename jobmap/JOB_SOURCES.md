# JobMap job sources

Updated 12 October 2026. This is the source register from our discussions and recent reviews, not a claim that every source has been fully checked in the current run. Each discovery run must record actual coverage, pagination, dates and access limits separately.

Current scope: all credible current technical employee choices, including part-time, full-time and fixed-term roles. Advertised hours, travel, payroll and material skill gaps stay visible. Prefer Cardiff, practical South Wales and genuine UK-remote work. No shortlist quota or latest-week-only exclusion. Generic off-field, unpaid, freelance/self-employed and future graduate campaigns remain excluded.
